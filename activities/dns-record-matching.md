# DNS Record Matching Challenge

Match each scenario with the best DNS term.

## Directions

Choose the best match from the answer bank for each question.

Write your answers on paper, in notes, or in a separate document. Do not look for an answer key on this page.

## Answer Bank

A. DKIM  
B. CNAME  
C. TTL  
D. AAAA  
E. SPF  
F. MX  
G. TXT  
H. A  
I. DMARC

## Questions

1. An administrator needs a hostname to point to an IPv4 address.

2. A company wants to identify which email servers are authorized to send messages on behalf of its domain.

3. An administrator wants `support.company.com` to act as an alias for another hostname.

4. A receiving mail server needs information to help verify the digital signature on an email message.

5. An administrator needs to control how long DNS information can remain cached before it should be requested again.

6. A company needs to identify the mail exchanger responsible for receiving email for its domain.

7. An administrator needs a hostname to point to an IPv6 address.

8. A company wants to publish a policy telling receiving mail systems what to do when email authentication fails.

9. A company needs to publish text information in DNS, such as information used for domain verification.
